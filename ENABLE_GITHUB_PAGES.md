# Turn on the live shareable URL

After `PUSH_TO_GITHUB.cmd` succeeds:

1. Open https://github.com/mrsalezman845/lawn-business-os
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Pages** in the left sidebar.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **GitHub Actions**.
5. Open the **Actions** tab and let the `Deploy Lawn Business OS to GitHub Pages` workflow finish.

Your app should then be available at:

**https://mrsalezman845.github.io/lawn-business-os/**

If the first deployment ran before Pages was enabled, open **Actions**, choose the Pages workflow, and click **Run workflow** once.
