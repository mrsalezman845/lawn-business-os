Lawn Business OS Privacy Policy

Effective: September 19, 2026

The app stores business information entered by users, including customer contact details, service addresses, pricing, job records, expenses, leads and marketing spend.

Local mode

When Shared Sync is disabled, data is stored locally on the device/browser.

Shared Sync

When enabled, business data is transmitted to the sync service configured by the business users so authorized partners using the same shared plan key can access the data.

Permissions

The base app does not require precise location, contacts, microphone, camera, health data or advertising identifiers.

Before public release

Replace this starter policy with the operating business legal name, contact email, production backend details, retention and deletion process, and a public HTTPS privacy URL.
