# Lawn Business OS — Android Install

GitHub Actions builds the Android app in the cloud, so you do not need Android Studio on your Windows PC just to make the test APK.

## Download the APK
1. Open the `lawn-business-os` repository on GitHub.
2. Click **Actions**.
3. Open **Build Android App**.
4. Open the newest run with a green check.
5. Scroll to **Artifacts**.
6. Download **Lawn-Business-OS-Android-APK**.
7. Unzip the downloaded artifact.
8. Transfer `Lawn-Business-OS.apk` to your Android phone and tap it.
9. If Android asks, allow installs from the browser/files app you used for the APK, then install.

This is a debug/test APK for you and your business partner. A public Google Play release should use a signed release Android App Bundle (AAB), Play App Signing, a finished privacy policy, and store listing assets.
